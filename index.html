How to measure

Waist: Wrap a flexible measuring tape around your natural waist, keeping it level and comfortable. Don't pull it tight.

Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat, with the tape level.

Inseam: Measure from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg on trousers that fit as you prefer.

Body measurements and garment measurements aren't directly interchangeable: fit depends on ease, stretch, cut, and construction.
