How to read a silhouette

Shape, rise, length, fabric, and construction describe different things. A pair of trousers can be high-rise, cropped, and wide-leg at the same time. A skirt can be pleated and A-line.

These drawings are simplified teaching diagrams, not sewing patterns. Real garments vary with fabric, ease, proportions, and styling.
